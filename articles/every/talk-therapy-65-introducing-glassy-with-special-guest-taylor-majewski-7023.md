Dan and Nathan welcome Taylor Majewski to the podcast to celebrate the launch of her brand new Every newsletter, Glassy. A weekly study of technology through the lens of gender, Glassy will see Taylor digging into the themes that have guided her career thus far. She sat down to talk about how tech and gender came to sit at the center of her work, why Every is the best place for her to write about these topics, and why founders shouldn't be defensive or shy away from difficult conversations.
