Good news: We raised $2 million—an amount large enough that our investors will answer the phone if we need them, and small enough that we can keep being weird.

The round was co-led by __Reid Hoffman__ and __StartingLine VC__, with participation from __Will England__ of Walleye Capital. It’s a unique structure: We're not taking it all at once; instead, we can draw it down when we need it. Call it a sip seed round.

AI helps us do more with less, so we don’t need a giant pile of cash. But a little money is nice, because it’ll let us try new experiments quickly and easily.

Now, we’ll get back to doing what we do best: Write and build at the frontier of AI.

You’ll see more of what you’ve come to expect from us. __Hands-on__, __day-zero__ model reviews. Deep-dive essays on how AI __reshapes work__ and __the self__. AI-powered tools like __Cora__, __Sparkle__, and __Spiral__ that help you live and work better.

If you’re curious, you can read more about what we’re up to in the * New York Times* today.

Thanks for being along for the ride with us. I can’t wait for __what comes next__.
