## How Do You Compete with Free?

*Evan Armstrong / Napkin Math*

Notion is the hottest productivity software on the planet, with an adoring community and a fresh $10B valuation. Will it be enough to beat Microsoft? In our top article this week, Evan tackles the eternal question: how does David beat Goliath? Notion is assembling a rebel alliance consisting of teenagers on TikTok, devs using their APIs, and novel automation partners. Will it work? Check out the article to see Evan's full prediction.

## Becoming a Better Storyteller

*Katie Parrott / Masterful Storytelling*

Last month, Every hosted a live event on our Discord with Planet Money co-founder, book author, and New Yorker writer Adam Davidson. Adam chatted with us about the rise of storytelling and the importance of improving our own skills as storytellers. In this piece, Katie Parrott compiled our notes from the conversation and boiled them down to 4 actionable items that will help readers tell better stories. Adam is a master of his craft, and this piece is absolutely worth your time.
