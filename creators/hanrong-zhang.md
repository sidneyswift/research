## Design philosophy (if discernible)

The studied paper favors a narrow intervention at the access interface: preserve the router and skill bodies, differentiate descriptions relative to neighbors, qualify names and evaluate both old-route retention and new-skill discoverability. This describes one coauthored work, not a generalized personal philosophy. [[sources/zhang--skill-plus-plus#method]] [[sources/zhang--skill-plus-plus#plasticity]]
