## Design philosophy

The source's distinctive combination is **human decision ownership plus substantial automated execution infrastructure**. It moves expensive choices into reviewable artifacts, keeps task context outside one conversation, and lets multiple coding agents share a collaboration surface. The important limit is that workflow guidance and hard runtime guarantees occupy different layers. [[sources/humanlayer--humanlayer#workflow-phases]], [[sources/humanlayer--humanlayer#task-model]], [[sources/humanlayer--humanlayer#auth-boundaries]]

The UI-copy guide makes a second philosophy explicit: explain consequences and next actions rather than narrating internal machinery. Its instruction to fix a defect without flattening the rest of a sentence is a useful counterweight to mechanical “AI cleanup.” These are authoring rules, not a claim that every shipped screen complies. [[sources/humanlayer--humanlayer#ui-copy]]
