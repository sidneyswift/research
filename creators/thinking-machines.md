## Design philosophy

Treat models as customizable substrates; measure learning as well as out-of-box performance, train long-horizon tool use, and stage openness based on evidence. Alignment and execution monitoring are separate parts of the reported model-development stack. The Bridgewater article is attributed to its own AIA Labs authors, not silently credited to Thinking Machines.
