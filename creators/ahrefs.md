# Ahrefs

> **One-line:** The largest SEO and web analytics tooling company; publishes large-scale data studies on search behavior that now extend into AI search optimization — relevant for understanding how AI chatbots discover and cite content.

*Page shape (gbrain's brain-page schema, per [[patterns/behavioral/diarization]] applied to ourselves): **compiled truth on top** (edited in place) + an **append-only `## Timeline`** below. Done when it passes the [page checklist](../_schemas/_definition-of-done.md).*

## Attributes

- **What they ship**: SEO tools (site audit, keyword research, backlink analysis, rank tracking), web-scale data studies, blog content
- **Distribution channel**: `ahrefs.com`, Ahrefs Blog, social media (Tim Soulo's personal accounts)
- **Position in ecosystem**: The dominant SEO tooling company, now extending research into AI search citation behavior. Not building AI agents or skills, but producing the best public data on how AI chatbots interact with web content — which informs how anyone building content (including agent docs, skill documentation, marketing) should think about discoverability.
- **Notable quotes / framings**: "28.3% of ChatGPT's most-cited pages have zero Google organic visibility. A completely separate discovery layer." — Tim Soulo, 2026-06-02

## Design philosophy (if discernible)

Data-first, claims-with-numbers. Ahrefs never publishes a "top 5 tips for AI SEO" listicle — they publish "we analyzed 1 billion data points and here's what the data says." The rigor is credible because they own one of the largest web crawl datasets outside of Google. Their commercial interest (selling SEO tools) is transparent but doesn't invalidate the data.
