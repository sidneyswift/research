## Why we cite this

The single most data-heavy public analysis of how AI chatbots cite web content. Relevant for:

- Understanding AI as a **parallel discovery layer** (separate from Google organic rankings)
- Quantifying the citation behavior differences between ChatGPT, AI Overviews, and AI Mode
- Informing content strategy for anyone building content that needs to be found by AI (including Recoup's marketing)
- Understanding the YouTube↔AI correlation (relevant to Recoup's music/video domain)
- Grounding claims about "AI SEO" with actual data rather than speculation
