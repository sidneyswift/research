About tibo.dog

tibo.dog is a free 2.5D pixel survivors-like by @poteto. It plays in your web browser and inside posts on X, with no download and no account.

How it plays

You play Tibo, the game's hero. Swarms of pests and bosses close in from every side, and stray dogs join Tibo's pack along the way, up to three at a time. Tibo's potato weapons fire on their own, so you move, dash and pick an upgrade at each level-up.

Play the Story, a short first game, or pick a level from Easy to Nightmare. In a level, survive 20 minutes to win, then keep going for as long as you last.

Controls

Keyboard

  • Move: W A S D or the arrow keys
  • Dash: Space
  • Pause: P or Escape
  • Level-up: a number key picks a card, and R, X and B reroll, skip or banish while you have some left

Touch

  • Move: drag anywhere
  • Dash: flick
  • Pause: the pause button
  • Level-up: tap a card, Reroll, Skip or Banish

Languages
