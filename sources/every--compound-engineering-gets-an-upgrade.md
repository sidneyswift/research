# "Compound Engineering Gets an Upgrade" (Kieran Klaassen, Every)

> Kieran Klaassen's 2026-05-29 essay narrating how compound engineering **grew from a
> four-step loop to an eight-step one** as models got more capable: the "work" phase
> "became boring — in the best way," so human judgment migrated to the two *ends* — a new
> **ideate/brainstorm** front (decide what's worth building) and a new **polish** back (does
> it feel right). The "sandwich" framing — *"AI is the stuff in the middle. Humans are the
> bread on either end"* — and the claim that the pattern generalizes to knowledge work.
> The dated companion to the living [[sources/every--compound-engineering]] guide.

## Anchor map

Thematic pointers into the essay's named claims (and the labeled snapshot fragments).
**Do not invent anchors beyond this list.**

- `#root` — the essay as a whole
- `#opening` — *"In its early days, compound engineering was mostly about the code. I wanted
  to see if I could get an AI model to make a plan, do the work the way I wanted it done,
  review the results against my standards, and incorporate lessons from my feedback…"*
- `#original-loop` — the original four steps: *"Brainstorm → work → review → compound →
  repeat."*
- `#eight-loop` — the expanded eight steps: *"Ideate → brainstorm → plan → work → review →
  polish → compound → repeat."*
- `#work-is-boring` — *"the work phase has become boring—in the best way. If the plan is good
  and the agent has the right context, it usually does the work right."* (Why the middle
  shrank and judgment moved to the ends.)
- `#sandwich` — *"AI is the stuff in the middle. Humans are the bread on either end, holding
  it together."*
- `#front-phases` — the new ideate/brainstorm front: *"I need to decide what is worth
  building. I need to understand the user, the product, the weird edge cases…"*
- `#polish` — the new polish back: *"I click around and look at the design. I read the copy.
  I ask whether the experience feels right."*
- `#knowledge-work` — *"The guide is about engineering, but I think the pattern applies to
  knowledge work much more broadly."*

## Why we cite this

- **The evolution narrative behind the loop** — explains *why* the plugin's loop has the
  shape it does (a front-loaded ideate/brainstorm and a back-loaded polish bookending a
  shrinking "work" middle). Grounds the "humans at the ends" framing in
  [[concepts/compound-engineering]]. ([[sources/every--compound-engineering-gets-an-upgrade#sandwich]],
  `#front-phases`, `#polish`)
- **The "work became boring" claim** — a concrete, datable read on capability progress: as
  models improve, the human-judgment bottleneck moves *outward*, not away.
  ([[sources/every--compound-engineering-gets-an-upgrade#work-is-boring]])
- **The generalization-to-knowledge-work claim** — positions compound engineering as a method
  beyond software, relevant to whether the [[concepts/compound-engineering]] pattern is
  domain-specific. ([[sources/every--compound-engineering-gets-an-upgrade#knowledge-work]])
